## What's new

### Settings

- A new **Settings** button at the bottom left of the sidebar holds the theme, **Dark pages**, your version of Compare and a **Check for updates** button.
- Settings tells you whether you're up to date, a new version is available, or the check couldn't finish. You can download and apply an update from there too.
- Settings is still in reach with the sidebar collapsed and at high zoom. Esc closes it.

### Themes

- A new **System** theme, now the default, follows your Windows setting and switches between light and dark as soon as Windows does, title bar buttons included.
- **Light**, **Dark** and **OLED** have moved from the top bar into Settings, with previews in each theme's real colors.
- **Dark pages** is in Settings too, and stays in sync with the moon button in the Document and Sheet views.
- All three themes now use Mica behind the sidebar and top bar, so text and switches stay readable whatever window is behind Compare.

### Easier to read and to use from the keyboard

- Text, line numbers, switches and accent buttons now meet WCAG AA contrast in Light, Dark and OLED.
- The view switch (such as Text and Document), the sheet tabs and the themes in Settings each take a single Tab stop. Move between them with the arrow keys, Home and End.
- In a narrow window, the option switches fold into an **Options** button, with a dot on it while any of them is on.

## Fixes

- When a file can't be opened, the side it was meant for says why in plain words, such as that the file is open in another program, instead of a system error with the full path. **Choose another file** lets you try again.
- If a side already had text, a file that fails to open over it no longer hides that text. A bar above it says what went wrong, and the text stays as it was.
- Error messages stay until you close them with **✕** or Esc, and a toast no longer covers a side's buttons by repeating the same error. Other messages don't go away while the pointer is on them.
- Saving an export and working out checksums also give plain messages when a file is locked or missing.

## Download

- **Compare-Setup-1.5.0.exe** installs the app and adds *Send to → Compare* to Explorer.
- **Compare-Portable-1.5.0.exe** runs without installing.

If you already have 1.3.0 or later, click **Update to 1.5.0** in the app, then **Restart to update** once the download finishes.
